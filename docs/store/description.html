Place. Merge. Rise.

Highrise is an isometric city-builder where same-level buildings merge, 2048 style. Place buildings on an isometric grid, slide them together to make a taller one, and spend your income to build more, boost your city, expand the land and move on to the next age.

How to play

  • Build: press BUILD (or Space) to put a new building on a free tile. It earns money on its own.
  • Merge: slide every building with WASD or the arrow keys. Two neighbors of the same level merge into one building of the next level, and higher levels earn more.
  • Boost: BOOST places a booster that earns nothing itself but raises the income of the buildings on the tiles it covers. Boosters merge with other boosters, and overlapping ones add up.
  • Expand: EXPAND grows your land so there is room for more buildings.
  • Next age: finish the current age's tasks, such as owning a building of a given level or earning a given income per second, then pay for NEXT AGE to unlock the next set of buildings.
  • Inspect and sell: switch to inspection mode, click a building to see its stats, or sell it.

Features

  • Eleven building levels, each with a visible change: a door, a window, a gate, another floor, a roof.
  • Four ages with their own look and their own build sounds.
  • Boosters with five levels and a different coverage in every age.
  • Age tasks: hover over NEXT AGE to see what is still missing.
  • Your city is saved. Quit any time and press CONTINUE later.
  • English and Turkish, with window, UI scale and audio settings.

Controls

  • Move and merge buildings: WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Build: the BUILD button or Space.
  • Boost, expand, next age: the BOOST, EXPAND and NEXT AGE buttons.
  • Inspect or sell a building: the INFORMATION button, then click a building.

Installing

Download the zip for your system, unzip it and run the game. No Python or installer needed.

  • Windows: run highrise.exe. The game isn't code-signed, so the first launch may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open Highrise.app. The app isn't signed, so macOS blocks the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to Highrise, then Open.
  • Linux: run ./highrise/highrise.
